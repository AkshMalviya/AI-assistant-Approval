# OomniEye Assistant Operations Manual

## Present me Summary
The "Present me Summary" feature allows the assistant to summarize the current queue of pending approvals out loud or in text, in the operator's language. The summary is strictly prioritized by urgency, ensuring that the most critical items are brought to your attention first.

## Talk to me
The "Talk to me" feature opens a free-form, multi-turn conversation about the approvals queue. You can ask open-ended questions such as "which of these needs my attention first and why?" and the assistant will help you navigate and understand your workload interactively.

## Help me
The "Help me" feature is designed to answer specific operational questions. Its answers are strictly grounded in this short reference document and the established dashboard policies, ensuring that the assistant does not hallucinate or rely on the model's general knowledge.

## Teach me
The "Teach me" feature acts as an interactive trainer. It walks a new operator step-by-step through how to review and act on an approval. It is highly adaptive, meaning the explanation will adjust dynamically if you ask follow-up questions during the walkthrough.

## Replay Greeting
The "Replay Greeting" feature regenerates a short, context-aware greeting. Instead of replaying a fixed string, it generates a fresh greeting that references the current state of the dashboard, such as how many items are currently pending in your queue right now.
